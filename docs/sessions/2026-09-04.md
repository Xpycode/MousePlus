## Decisions

- Reveal remains visible until the HUD invocation closes. Immediate mouse-exit collapse was rejected because normal travel from a parent toward its outer child would make the arc unstable.
- Reveal is genuinely hover-driven: center-to-parent traversal opens the branch without click or release; Always retains deliberate click/release expansion and Hidden never exposes the branch.
- Hold-release commits from the trigger event's pointer snapshot rather than callback timing or stale SwiftUI hover state.
- Label visibility and orientation resolve as a presentation-layer pure function decoupled from the persisted model type, so the parallel model (1.1) and presentation (1.2) tasks needed no reconciliation once both landed — `LabelPresentationPolicy` binds directly to the model's `LabelOrientation`.
- The Menu/Inner/Middle/Outer customization tab is intentionally separate UI state from `model.activeBand`, per the Wave 1 Operational Learning that the Menu Items band selector must never be silently retargeted by browsing ring settings. It is not lifted into `MenuEditorModel`; it resets to the Menu tab whenever the outer "Menu & Rings / Selected Item" inspector switch leaves and returns, which is acceptable since no acceptance criterion requires it to persist.
- Task 3.2 needed no production code: `hudCustomization` (and therefore its new label fields) was already carried as one Codable struct through `merged(into:)`, the coordinator's field-based dirty/merge/save machinery, and reset/undo/backup-restore — none of it decomposes into individual sub-fields. Verification work belonged entirely in tests, not implementation.
